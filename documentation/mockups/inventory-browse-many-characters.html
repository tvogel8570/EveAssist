Inventory

Sample data: names are made up, and items are spread over 21 characters at random. Item types, categories, stations and the way items sit in containers and ships are real. The "account" of a character is a tag you give it, because EVE does not say which game account a character is on. Items fitted to ships and in ship holds are counted in the totals: use the How filter to leave them out.
